About

I work on making AI systems more reliable and computationally efficient. My interests lie at the intersection of LLM agents and post-training, sequential decision-making, and statistical learning.

I currently conduct research with Jiayu Chen at the University of Hong Kong on LLM-agent search and credit assignment, and with Lixu Wang at CUHK-Shenzhen on machine unlearning and weak-to-strong transfer. Previously, I studied Applied Mathematics (with a minor in Computer Science) at Southeast University and spent Fall 2025 as a visiting student at UC Berkeley.

Research interests: Reliable and efficient AI · LLM agents · RL and credit assignment · Statistical decision theory · Game theory

Current Research

Decision-Guided Relative Temporal Credit & Causal Repair (DG-RTC)

Temporal credit assignment for multi-step agents, using decision-conditioned counterfactual evaluation and causal repair to improve post-training decisions.

Cross-Model Functional Causal Support (CFCS)

Transferring causal evidence across model scales to identify useful intervention locations in larger language models.

OpenPAC: Certificate-Gated Learning for LLM Agents

Using verifiable evidence and clean–adversarial training pairs to reduce learning from unverified or misleading feedback.

From Deliberation to Instinct

Learning state-dependent policies that decide when an agent should search, inspect, test, use tools, or stop under a compute budget.

Selected Publications & Manuscripts

Research manuscripts are clearly distinguished from accepted conference publications. The summaries below follow my October 2026 CV.

  1. PAC-CF: Calibrating Irreversible Frontier Pruning in LLM-Guided Search
    Tianhao Qian, Jiayu Chen, Z. Sun, Lixu Wang
    2026 · Manuscript under review · arXiv
    Calibrated pruning of LLM-guided search frontiers. In ToolTree at budget 100: +4.38 percentage points in full-test utility, with 18.95% fewer requests and 23.76% fewer tokens in the reported setting.
  2. Relative Kinetic Utility: Calibrating Cross-Layer Credit for Global Structured LLM Pruning
    Tianhao Qian, Guilin Qi, Jiayu Chen
    2026 · Manuscript under review · arXiv
    A label-free cross-layer channel importance score. At 40% sparsity on Qwen2.5-7B, +3.79 Mean3 points over GISP in the reported questions-only calibration experiment.
  3. Storage Is Not Strategy: State-Conditioned Support Control for LLM Unlearning
    Tianhao Qian, Ziming Hong, Chongyang Gao, Kezhen Chen, Lixu Wang
    2026 · Manuscript under review · arXiv
    Separates knowledge localization from useful intervention selection. A storage-localization signal reached AUROC 0.981 but agreed with the better intervention on only 17/36 targets.
  4. Alternating Gradient Flow Utility: A Unified Metric for Structural Pruning and Dynamic Routing in Deep Networks
    Tianhao Qian, Zhuoxuan Li, Jinde Cao, Xinli Shi, Leszek Rutkowski
    Research 2025 · Preprint 2026 · arXiv
    Gradient-flow-based structural pruning and dynamic routing, including high-compression experiments on vision networks.
  5. Large Language Model for Discrete Optimization Problems: Evaluation and Step-by-step Reasoning
    Tianhao Qian, Guilin Qi, Z. Y. Wu, Ran Gu, Xuanyi Liu, Canchen Lyu
    Research 2025 · Preprint 2026 · arXiv
    Evaluation of LLM performance on discrete optimization problems, including limits of chain-of-thought prompting.
  6. Resource-constrained Amazons Chess Decision Framework Integrating Large Language Models and Graph Attention
    Tianhao Qian, Zhuoxuan Li, Jinde Cao, Xinli Shi, Leszek Rutkowski
    Research 2024 · Preprint 2026 · arXiv
    Resource-constrained Monte Carlo Tree Search with graph-based filtering of imperfect LLM-generated supervision; 66.5% win rate against GPT-4o-mini at a 50-node budget.
  7. Taxonomy Induction Using LLMs: An Enhanced Framework by Integrating Doubly-Checked Mechanism and Self-Evaluation Strategy
    J. Li, Y. Meng, Lixu Wang, Tianhao Qian, S. Zhai, Guilin Qi
    2024 · Accepted, CCKS-IJCKG 2024 · Proceedings
    Iterative validation and self-evaluation for more reliable LLM-assisted taxonomy induction.
